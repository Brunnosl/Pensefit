# Imagens das receitas

Geradas com a ferramenta imagegen integrada (modo built-in), sem chave de API no aplicativo. São imagens ilustrativas; não representam consumo registrado, medidas nutricionais ou uma refeição do usuário. Os quatro arquivos foram copiados para o projeto, sem substituir as mídias originais dos exercícios.

## recipe-rice.png

PNG original: `media-source/recipe-rice.png`.

Prompt utilizado:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: illustrative recipe photograph for the PENSE NUTRI fitness app. Create a square food photograph, natural premium editorial style, of a modest matte dark ceramic plate containing ONLY cooked fluffy white rice and moist shredded roasted chicken breast, arranged in two distinct neat portions. No vegetables, sauces, toppings, herbs, oil bottle, cutlery or other foods. The exact recipe is 'Arroz com frango desfiado'. Camera slightly above at 45 degrees, close composition, entire plate centered with room around the rim on dark charcoal stone table. Soft natural side light, realistic food texture, appetizing but not exaggerated. No people, text, brands, packaging, watermark. This is an illustrative image, not a nutrition measurement. 1024 square.
